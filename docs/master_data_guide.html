01 / Read the page correctly

Screen map

What is connected, and what is illustrative

Master data is the reusable foundation that tells MRP what can be planned and how materials relate. The page has navigation and upload controls, but not all displayed actions are connected to tenant storage yet.

Working tenant-backed path
Choose a tenant → choose an upload type → upload a correctly formatted CSV. The server validates columns and references, then writes to that tenant's schema. Success toast gives the uploaded row count.
Demo-only display path
Grid rows, owners, statuses, KPI totals, last-sync time, data-quality score, Add/Edit, Toggle status, and Export are local sample UI. They do not read or modify tenant master tables.

Counts across hard-coded sample arrays. They do not measure uploaded tenant rows.

02 / The five data domains

What each domain is for

The left panel groups records by planner task

The five visible domains are screen categories, not five physical database tables. Here are the sample items shown and the real source used by MRP.

1 • Materials

Purchased or made components

Defines item identity, unit, cost, scrap, sourcing, replenishment and capacity defaults. MRP needs these values to calculate and size supply.

Demo: RM-0045 Aluminum Sheet, MT-0012 Motor Assembly, PK-3011 Packaging Box, SP-7781 Screw M6, RM-1120 Copper Wire, EL-2204 Control Relay.
Actual source: Material master CSV
2 • Products & BOMs

Finished goods and recipes

Finished goods receive forecast demand. A BOM links each product to component materials and quantities per product unit.

Demo products: PRD-1001 Control Panel, PRD-1002 Switch Assembly, PRD-1003 Power Module, PRD-1004 Enclosure Unit.
Actual sources: Finished goods + Bill of materials CSVs
3 • Suppliers

Vendor reference and performance

Supplier identity, lead time, terms, quality and delivery performance can support purchasing decisions.

Demo: SUP-001 ABC Suppliers, SUP-002 Global Traders, SUP-004 PackRight Solutions.
Current state: demo only; no Supplier upload/CRUD path here
4 • Locations

Sites and facilities

Locations shown in the page are sample warehouses and plants. MRP uses a plant code to scope stock, products, BOM, demand and constraints.

Demo: LOC-001 Main Warehouse, LOC-002 Plant North, LOC-003 Plant Central.
Actual MRP source: Plant master CSV
5 • Planning Parameters

Replenishment policies

Safety stock, maximum capacity, lead time, minimum order and order increment influence when and how much to plan.

Demo: Default service level, Planning time fence, Default calendar.
Actual MRP sources: Material master + Plant constraints CSV
Also needed

Operational planning inputs

Inventory provides opening stock and receipts. Demand Planning provides forecast by finished good and period. These are not Master Data domains.

Actual sources: Inventory CSV + monthly/weekly demand CSV or scenario workbook
Do not confuse sample names with company records. The codes and people shown above are seeded in the frontend source file for screen demonstration. They do not come from SAP, ERP, a supplier feed, or the selected tenant database.

03 / CSV fields that define the foundation

Plant + material master

Upload these before dependent files
Plant master answers: where will planning take place? Material master answers: what item is needed, in what unit, and with what supply policy?

Plant master

CSV fieldMeaning and planning use
plant_codeStable facility key referenced by finished goods, BOM, inventory, constraints and demand.
plant_nameReadable facility label.
country_codeThree-letter country code; supports country filtering and currency context. Country must be configured or supplied using supported metadata.
locationDescriptive city, region or site location.
active_flagtrue/false. Inactive facilities are excluded from planning options.

Material master

CSV fieldMeaning and planning use
material_codeUnique tenant item key referenced by BOM, inventory and plant constraints.
descriptionReadable material name.
categoryRaw, Packing, or Intermediate. Classifies and filters requirements.
supply_typeBUY = procure externally. MAKE = manufacture internally; a MAKE material may have its own BOM.
uomStock/planning unit such as ton, kg, or each. Keep consistent with BOM and inventory quantities.
unit_costCost per unit used in planned procurement value and spend reporting.
scrap_pctLoss fraction from 0 up to but not including 1; 0.02 means 2% extra consumption.
lead_time_bucketsNumber of weekly or monthly buckets to replenish; interpretation follows run bucket type.
moqMinimum order quantity applied when there is a net requirement.
order_multipleQuantity increment to round an order up to (for example, 100-unit increments).
safety_stock_minDefault projected inventory floor. Plant constraints can override it.
max_capacityDefault stock/capacity ceiling for capacity alerts; plant constraints can override it.
Units must agree. A BOM quantity expressed as each cannot be applied safely to a material stored in kg unless the source data defines the conversion consistently.

04 / Product structure + policy

Finished goods, BOM, constraints

These connect demand to supply

Finished goods

CSV fieldMeaning and planning use
plant_codePlant where the product is planned/made; must exist first.
fg_codeFinished-good key used by demand and BOM parent rows.
descriptionReadable product name.
product_typeBulk or Bagged; supports product-type filtering.
uomForecast/output unit, coherent with demand and BOM ratio.
active_flagtrue/false; only active products are selectable for planning.

Bill of materials (BOM)

CSV fieldMeaning and planning use
plant_codePlant-specific product structure.
fg_codeParent finished good; must exist for the same plant.
component_material_codeChild material consumed by the product; must exist in Material master.
quantity_per_fgQuantity of component needed per one finished-good unit; the core BOM ratio.
quantity_uomSource unit for the component ratio; use the material's unit.
scrap_factorOptional source field; current importer/calculation does not use it. Scrap is applied from Material master `scrap_pct`.
BOM upload replaces included parents. The importer deletes existing component rows for each finished-good parent found in the file, then inserts the supplied rows. Upload all component lines for each included parent, not a partial delta.

Plant constraints

CSV fieldMeaning and planning use
plant_code + material_codeThe plant/material pair the rule belongs to; both must exist.
min_stock_qtyPlant-level projected stock floor (the safety-stock target in forward MRP).
reorder_levelStored reorder-point reference. It is not used by the current forward netting formula.
max_stock_qtyMaximum stock reference and fallback capacity limit.
max_storage_capacityStorage limit used for capacity alert when provided.
lead_time_bucketsPlant/material lead-time override; material default is fallback.
moqPlant/material minimum-order override.
order_multiplePlant/material order-increment override.
uomOptional reference unit for constraint quantities.

Generic demo parameters such as service level, planning time fence and calendar are not currently uploaded or applied by this forward MRP engine.

05 / How the records become a plan

Upload-to-MRP flow

For one selected tenant
1. Plantfacility + country
→
2. Materialsitems + supply rules
→
3. Finished goodsforecastable products
4. Constraintsplant-level policy
→
5. BOMcomplete component recipe
→
6. Inventoryon-hand + receipts
→
7. Demandforecast by product/period
8. Run MRPone tenant, selected scenario
→
9. Review planorders, dates, risks

Order matters because database references are checked. Plant must exist before finished goods. Plant and product/material references must exist before BOM, inventory, constraints or demand imports.

Forecast x BOM ratio x (1 + material scrap) = gross component requirementThe engine aggregates component requirements for each plant, material and time bucket.

Netting and replenishment

For each bucket, MRP uses projected stock plus scheduled and dated in-transit receipts, then subtracts gross requirement. If projected stock would fall below the minimum, the difference becomes net requirement. Suggested quantity observes MOQ and order multiple; lead time determines release date.

Alerts and limits

Projected stock above configured capacity raises a capacity alert. An order whose release is due by the first bucket can be flagged for expedite. Maximum capacity is checked, not used to cap the recommended order quantity.

Current formula boundaries: reorder_level is stored but not used in forward netting. Reserved stock is displayed in Inventory but is not deducted by this MRP formula. BOM `scrap_factor` is ignored; material `scrap_pct` is used.

What to check after upload

  • Confirm the correct tenant is selected and every upload reports success.
  • Upload complete parent BOMs and check that all product/material codes match exactly.
  • Check inventory and forecast units and dates before running.
  • Use Dashboard to select country, plant, horizon, bucket type, planning start date, and one demand scenario.
  • Review time-phased material rows, planned order quantity, release date, projected stock, expedite, and capacity flags.
Recommended starting order: Plant master → Material master → Finished goods → Plant constraints → Bill of materials → Inventory & stock → Demand forecast/scenario → Run MRP.
